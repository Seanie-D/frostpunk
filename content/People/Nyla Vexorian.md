title: Nyla Vexorian
---

**Guild Seer. Keeper of the Hearth Engine.**

---

## Who She Is

Nyla Vexorian is the most knowledgeable living person on the subject of the Hearth Engine - possibly the most knowledgeable person, full stop.

She trained in what could be learned about the traditions of Thalorin Vex, the archmage who predicted the Frost and whose work forms the theoretical backbone of everything the Guild now maintains. The Engine's operation, its calibration, its arcane requirements - Nyla understands all of it, and she and Luthor are collaboratively the reason it hasn't failed. She also uses the **Seer's Orb**, a divinatory instrument, to locate concentrations of relic energy in the Wastes. When the Guild sends you somewhere, there's a good chance Nyla is the one who found it.

---

## Appearance

Fiendborn and frizzy-haired, with a pale, indoor-worn complexion that suggests she hasn't spent much time outside in years. Her fingers are perpetually stained with alchemical burns and ink. Her robes are layered, practical, and covered in margin notes. She adjusts them constantly, fidgets with the cuffs, moves her hands even when she's standing still.

---

## Personality

Gentle and a little nervous. She talks fast when she gets excited about something - and she gets excited about almost everything theoretically interesting, which is a lot. Ask her a simple question and you might get a twenty-minute answer, but it'll be worth it.

She's not cold, just focused. Her and Luthor can get into a flow-state when they're running maintenance on the Engine. The Engine is her responsibility and she takes that weight seriously - sometimes too seriously. She avoids eye contact unless she's deep in an explanation, and she gets visibly uncomfortable when people treat relics as interchangeable fuel rather than objects worth understanding.

---

## Her Role in the Guild

Nyla keeps the Engine running and tells the Guild where to look. She briefs expeditions on what they're likely to find at a site and what to prioritize recovering. If you bring back something unusual - an artifact with strange properties, a manuscript in a language nobody recognizes - she's the first person to talk to.

You'll find her in the Engine chamber or the archive room adjacent to it. She's always there. It's not entirely clear she goes back to her Guild quarters, because the Engine chamber may as well BE her quarters.
