The Hearth Engine is the beating heart of the town of Hearth. It is an ancient, arcane construct of unknown origin, discovered by the first settlers who fled the Eternal Frost.

## How it Works
The Engine creates an invisible "Thermal Sphere" around the town, keeping the temperature habitable and resisting any Arcane Frost. However, its magical fuel is constantly depleting. To keep the flame alive, it must be fed with **Magical Relics**.

### Fueling the Engine
- **Relics:** Any magical item or artifact recovered from the wastes can be sacrificed to the Engine.
- **Lore Manuscripts:** While not as potent as relics, ancient manuscripts containing arcane secrets can be "processed" (2 manuscripts = 1 minor relic's worth of fuel).
