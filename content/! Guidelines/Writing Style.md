# Wiki Writing Style Guide

Guidelines for all content written in the Frostpunk Player Wiki. Applies to Claude-generated content and any manual edits.

---

## The Golden Rule

**Never use em-dashes ( -- or ---) in Wiki content.**

This means no constructions like:
- *"The Engine -- Hearth's only source of warmth -- must be fed weekly."*
- *"Relics are the currency of survival---bring them back."*

Use alternatives instead:

| Instead of em-dash | Use |
|--------------------|-----|
| Parenthetical aside | Parentheses: *"The Engine (Hearth's only source of warmth) must be fed weekly."* |
| Abrupt break or pivot | A new sentence or a colon: *"The Engine must be fed weekly. It is Hearth's only source of warmth."* |
| Appositive clause | Commas: *"The Engine, Hearth's only source of warmth, must be fed weekly."* |

---

## Voice & Tone

- **Direct and grounded.** This is a hard world. Prose should feel like it earns its words.
- **Second person where appropriate.** Wiki pages addressing players directly use "you" (e.g., "Your character begins with...").
- **Present tense for world descriptions.** The world exists now; write as if the reader is already in it.
- **No irony or winking.** The setting is played straight. Jokes belong at the table, not in the wiki.

---

## Formatting

- Use **bold** for game terms, faction names, and mechanical keywords on first use in a section.
- Use *italics* for in-world quotes, flavor text, or NPC speech snippets.
- Callout blocks (`> [!info]`, `> [!tip]`, etc.) are for player-facing notes and tips only -- not lore.
- Keep headers short. If a header needs a subtitle, reconsider the structure.
- Tables for mechanical reference (costs, stats, comparisons). Prose for lore and flavor.

---

## What Goes in the Wiki

- **Spoiler-free.** Nothing that reveals unfound relic sites, hidden faction agendas, or GM-only lore.
- **Approved content only.** No page goes live without Sean's sign-off (except structural/formatting edits).
- **Player-relevant.** If a player can't act on the information, it probably doesn't belong here.
