The world has passed through six distinct Ages. Each left its mark on the ruins you'll explore, the relics you'll recover, and the ground beneath Hearth itself.

---

## The Six Ages

### Age I — The Myriad Deep
*Oldest known era. Pre-human.*

Cyclopean civilizations of unknown origin shaped the world through primal force. Their architecture dwarfs anything built since. Most of what they built is buried, submerged, or simply too large to recognize as construction.

Their purpose is unknown. Their end is unknown.

---

### Age II — The Age of the Skylords
*A floating empire. A catastrophic fall.*

A civilization mastered the skies — cities, fortresses, and trade routes suspended in the air by forces no one has since replicated. Their hubris brought them down, literally. The wreckage of their fall is scattered across the world.

One structure from this Age is still visible from the ground, intact against all reason.

---

### Age III — The Dark Ages
*Classic kingdoms. Dead gods.*

A long era of mortal kingdoms, wars, and the worship of gods who no longer answer. The polytheistic pantheon of this Age is gone — whether killed, departed, or simply silent depends on who you ask. Their temples remain.

---

### Age IV — The Magitech Dominion
*Industry and arcane power, fused.*

Magic became industrialized. Factories ran on ley lines. Cities hummed with enchanted infrastructure. This is the Age that produced **Archmage Thalorin Vex** — the greatest artificer of recorded history, and the builder of the **Hearth Engine**.

The Engine was Vex's masterwork: a device capable of generating and sustaining a Thermal Sphere over a populated area, keeping it warm indefinitely. He built it in Hearth.

Nobody knows why he chose Hearth specifically.

---

### Age V — The Age of Cydonia
*The great bureaucracy. Skyships lost.*

A vast civilization of bureaucrats, administrators, and skyship navigators covered much of the known world. Cydonia was organized, sprawling, and ultimately fragile. Their skyships — the pride of the Age — vanished when the Frost came. Their ruins are still navigable, if frozen.

---

### Age VI — The Age of Frost
*Now. Year 10.*

The world froze. Not gradually — the Frost came within a generation, and it has not stopped. The cause is unknown. The arcane ice that covers the world is not natural; it is magical in origin, and it is permanent.

Hearth survives because of the Hearth Engine. Everywhere else does not.

**Key events:**

| Year | Event |
|------|-------|
| Year 0 | The Deep Freeze begins. The world goes cold. |
| Years 1–2 | Survivors abandon wheeled transport; sled networks replace roads. |
| Year 3–5 | Refugees converge on Hearth as word of the Engine spreads. |
| Year 10 | **Current date: March 3, Year 10.** The Engine holds. For now. |
