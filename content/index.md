The world is cold. Not just winter-cold, but a deep, arcane, soul-chilling ice that put civilization in the fridge, permanently. You are a member of the **[[The Guild|Guild]]**, a group of brave (or desperate) heroes who venture into the frozen wastes to recover magical relics and fuel the ancient **[[The Hearth Engine]]** - the only thing keeping the town of [[Hearth]] alive.

## The Core Session Loop

1. **Preparation:** Start in Hearth, pick a Relic Site from the [[Mission Hub]], and gear up with rations and supplies.
2. **The Journey:** Travel across the frozen wastes using Wilderness Turns (days of travel).
3. **The Relic Site:** Explore ruined laboratories, ancient shrines, dungeons, and abandoned vaults. Locate and extract the magical artifacts you came for.
4. **The Return:** Bring the loot back to Hearth.
5. **Fueling the Flame:** Sacrifice at least one relic to the Hearth Engine. The more you burn, the more the Guild can grow.

## Key Mechanics

- **Inventory Slots:** 10 + STR modifier. You track your own.
- **Rations:** 1 per Wilderness Turn. 1 slot = 3 rations.
- **Relics:** The primary goal. Feed the Engine, keep Hearth alive.
- **Cold & Exposure:** Without warmth, the frost kills you.

## New Players — Start Here

**Step 1 — Join the Discord**

The Discord is where sessions are scheduled, questions get answered, and everything important is pinned. Get the invite link from Sean before anything else.

**Step 2 — Create Your Character**

Head to [[Character Creation]] and work through both pages: [[Thinking Up Your Character]] first, then [[Character Sheet Setup]] to build your sheet on [Nimbound](https://nimboundv2.com/). Send your answers to the GM's questions to Sean when you're done.

**Step 3 — Schedule Your First Session**

Once your character is ready, message Sean directly to get on the calendar. Your first session will include a short one-on-one opening scene before you join the rest of the group.
