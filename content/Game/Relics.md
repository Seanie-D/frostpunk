---
title: Relics
---

> *An object that survived when everything else didn't. Handle accordingly.*

Relics are magical artifacts from the previous Ages of the world - objects imbued with power during eras when magic and technology were far more advanced than anything that exists today. They are the primary objective of every Guild expedition, and the reason [[Hearth (Town)|Hearth]] is still alive.

---

## What They Are

A relic can be almost anything: a device, a weapon, a container, a piece of infrastructure, an object whose original purpose is completely unknown. What they share is that they carry residual magical energy from the Age that produced them - energy the [[The Hearth Engine|Hearth Engine]] can convert into warmth.

Most relics recovered from [[The Wastes]] date to [[The Six Ages#Age IV - The Magitech Dominion|Age IV]], when magical technology was at its peak. Older relics - from Age II or Age III - are rarer and typically more powerful. Age V relics exist but are common and weak; the Cydonian Bureaucracy didn't produce much of lasting magical value.

---

## Why They Matter

The [[The Hearth Engine]] consumes one relic per week to maintain the Thermal Sphere over Hearth. Miss a feeding and the Sphere begins to degrade. Let it go long enough and the cold comes back.

This is not theoretical. It is the reason the Guild exists.

---

## Finding Them

Relics are recovered from relic sites - ruins, vaults, laboratories, and other remnants of previous Ages buried across the Wastes. Each site is different. Some have been picked over already. Some have never been touched. What you find depends on where you go and how thoroughly you look.

See the [[Mission Hub]] for active leads.

---

## Bringing Them Back

Recovered relics are delivered to the **Engine Chamber** in the [[Hearth (Town)#The Guildhouse (The Old Fort)|Guildhouse]]. **Nyla Vexorian**, the Guild's Seer and Engine Keeper, appraises each one: its Age of origin, its estimated fuel value, and whether it has properties worth noting before it goes in.

The Guild logs every delivery.

---

## Burn or Keep

Not every relic has to go into the Engine. Some carry properties that make them useful as equipment - a weapon, a piece of armor, a tool with an active magical effect. Guild members can petition to keep a relic rather than burn it.

The Engine still needs feeding regardless. Keeping a relic means something else has to cover its fuel value.

---

## Manuscripts

Manuscripts are a secondary fuel source - written records, logs, and texts from previous Ages that carry enough residual magical charge to be useful. They burn less efficiently than relics: **two manuscripts equal one minor relic** in fuel value. They are worth recovering but cannot substitute for relics entirely.
