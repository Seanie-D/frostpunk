## Step 2 - Create Your Character Sheet on Nimbound

Build your character at **[Nimbound](https://nimboundv2.com/)** — the digital character sheet platform for this campaign.

**Your character has four things to pick:**
- **Ancestry** - [[Ancestries List|Browse ancestries here]]
- **Class** - [[Classes List|Browse classes here]]
- **Background** - [[Backgrounds List|Browse backgrounds here]]
- **Stats** - STR, DEX, INT, WIL. Ask the GM how stats are generated for this campaign.

Once your Nimbound sheet is complete, move on to Step 3.

---

## Step 3 - Initialize Your Inventory

Your inventory lives in the **Frostpunk Inventory Manager**, not Nimbound. Here's how to set it up:

1. Go to [frostpunk.lovable.app](https://frostpunk.lovable.app) and log in with your Discord account.
2. Add a row for each item in your Nimbound starting inventory, transferring it over one by one.

From this point on, the **Inventory Manager is your source of truth** for everything you're carrying. Nimbound is still useful for your stats, abilities, and class features - but inventory lives in the app.

> [!tip] Inventory Slots
> You have **10 + STR modifier** inventory slots. **1 slot = 3 rations.** Guild Vault storage is unlimited.

## Leveling Up

This campaign uses **milestone leveling**. Your entire Division levels up together - there is no individual XP. The GM will announce when a milestone is reached.

**Starting level:** All new characters begin at **Level 1** as Guild recruits. Upon completing your first proving period and becoming a full-fledged Guild member, the whole Division advances to **Level 2**. From there, milestones are tied to campaign events the GM tracks.
