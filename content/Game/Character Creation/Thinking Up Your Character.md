## Who Are You? (Adventuring Motivation)

The world is dangerous. Nobody goes out into the frost without a reason. Pick one of the following - or invent your own in conversation with the GM.

- I owe a life debt to someone in my party.
- I owe a lot of money to some very dangerous people.
- I need to grow in power to defeat someone who wronged me.
- I'm searching for a way to bring a loved one back from the dead.
- I'm trying to get back home - wherever that is.
- I'm searching for the one who stole something from me.
- I was polymorphed into another kind of creature by a wizard.
- Duty calls. I'm honor-bound to serve.
- I was best friends with - and betrayed by - someone.
- I'm lost (physically, emotionally, or spiritually).
- Wanderlust. I want to see what's out there.
- My home is in danger.
- Curiosity. I want to learn the deep secrets.
- I have something to prove.
- I'm following a prophecy.

> [!tip] Mix & Match
> A great motivation has connections to the world, to other party members, or to the people you'll meet. Talk with the GM - they have ideas.

## Your Story in Hearth

Answer these in writing - as much or as little as you like. Bullet points are fine. The GM needs these before Session 1, and they'll inform your opening scene.

**1. How did you arrive in Hearth, and when?**

*Hearth is the last warm place in a frozen world. Nobody stumbles here by accident - the roads are gone, the snow is lethal, and the Hearth Engine's warmth is the only reason the town exists. You made it here somehow. How?*

Some starting points:
- Were you already a resident, born or settled here before the Frost deepened?
- Did you come with a caravan, a faction, or a desperate group of refugees?
- Did someone guide you here - and do you still owe them?
- Did you arrive recently, or have you been here for years?

**2. What drives you to sign up for your first expedition?**

*The Relic Hunters Guild takes in new blood for a reason: the work is dangerous, and not everyone comes back. Why are you walking into that? Why now?*

Some starting points:
- Coin - the Guild pays well, and you need it.
- The Engine - you understand what happens if the relics stop coming.
- A specific relic - you've heard rumors of something out there that matters to you personally.
- Someone you trust vouched for the Guild, or someone you owe is already in it.
- You've got nothing left to lose - or nothing left to stay for.
