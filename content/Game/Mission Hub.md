# Mission Hub

The Guild, utilizing its Seer, scries the frozen wastes from the depths of the Guildhall, identifying potential Relic Sites. Pick a mission to begin your journey.

## Active Missions (The Board)

> [!info] **The Lost Expedition**
> **Source:** [[Nyla Vexorian]] / Guild Records
> **Relics Rumored:** **Goggles of Night**
> **Gameplay Length:** 1 - 2 Hours
> **Description:** A Guild auto-sled went missing in Year 4 - one day from home, never arrived. The Seer felt a single pulse off the eastern road. Whatever they were bringing back is still lashed to the rack.

> [!info] **The Steam-Valve Relay**
> **Source:** [[Nyla Vexorian]]
> **Relics Rumored:** **The Brass-Heart Compass**
> **Gameplay Length:** 2 - 4 Hours
> **Description:** A rhythmic metallic clanking echoes from a jagged iron tower in the Western Wastes. Nyla's Orb is revealing a powerful navigation tool within this ancient Magitech relay. Recovery is high priority.

> [!info] **The Maw's Respite**
> **Source:** [[Luthor Shrike]] / White Maw Scout
> **Relics Rumored:** **The Storm-Hide Buckler**
> **Gameplay Length:** 2 - 4 Hours
> **Description:** A White Maw defector has spoken of a sacred ice cave where a legendary shield made of beast-hide is kept. We're interested in this relic, but a warning for you: the cave is likely a site of significance for local clan splinter groups.

> [!info] **The Fallen Giant**
> **Source:** [[Nyla Vexorian]] / Scavenger Report
> **Relics Rumored:** **Boots of the Winterlands**, **Sentinel Shield**, **Iron Tankard**
> **Gameplay Length:** 2 - 4 Hours
> **Description:** A massive Magitech transport mecha - a towering relic of the Old World's industrial might - has been rediscovered half-buried in a frozen ravine in the Eastern Wastes. The Seer has felt a triple-pulse of resonance from within the wreckage. Exactly three significant relics remain dormant inside the six-legged titan's armored hull.
